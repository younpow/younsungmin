The time we spend together accumulates through the repetition of everyday life rather than through extraordinary events. Within the same room, objects shift, light changes, weather passes beyond the window, and the child changes little by little.

The time in these photographs does not belong to the child alone. It is also ours—the time we have lived together in the same space.

At some moments the child is present; at others, absent. Yet traces of our time together remain in the space.

Our Time is a record of shared time: time that continues to accumulate even as it passes.
